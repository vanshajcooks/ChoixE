Welcome to CampusConnect

Centralized Campus Placement Management Portal for students, recruiters, and placement officers.

Java 21 Spring Boot 3.4.2 MySQL 8 Spring Security
Student Portal
Undergraduates & Postgraduates
  • Self-registration & profile management
  • Academic details & resume uploads
  • Per-job automated eligibility check
  • Job applications & interview schedules
  • College placement analytics visibility
Recruiter Portal
Companies & HR Representatives
  • Company registration & profile
  • Job postings with custom criteria
  • Review applicants for own jobs
  • Schedule interviews & notify candidates
  • Update recruitment review results
Placement Officer
Administrative Oversight
  • Verify or reject student profiles
  • Approve or reject company registrations
  • Review & publish job opportunities
  • Publish college announcements
  • Drive placement analytics & metrics
